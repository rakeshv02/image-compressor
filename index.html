Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?
No — all compression happens in your browser using HTML Canvas. Your images never leave your device.
What formats are supported?
JPEG, PNG and WebP. The compressed output is saved as JPEG or WebP depending on your browser.
Is this free?
Yes — completely free, no sign-up needed.
Works on mobile?
Yes — works on any device. You can select images from your camera roll on iOS and Android.
